Clear about what goes where

WordLens runs on your own computer. Here is exactly what stays and what is sent.

Stays on your machine

  • Your PDF file
  • Your saved words and progress
  • Your API key

Sent when you click a word

  • The word and its sentence, to the AI provider you choose
  • Without a key, just the word, to a free dictionary and translation service

Questions

Do I need an API key?

No. Without a key you get a dictionary meaning and Hindi translation. With a free Gemini or Groq key you also get easy explanations, Hinglish, examples and sentence simplifying.

Does it work with scanned PDFs?

Not yet. The PDF needs selectable text. OCR support is on the roadmap.

Is it free?

WordLens itself is free and open source. The free tiers of Gemini and Groq are enough for normal reading.

Where are my saved words stored?

In a file called data/words.json inside the project folder. You can back it up or edit it.
